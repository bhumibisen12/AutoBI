# Visual Design Rules: Page Layout and Visual Density

## Maximum Visuals per Page
- Cap every report page at **6 to 8 visuals total**, comprising at most **4 KPI cards** in the top summary strip and **3 to 4 analytical charts or tables** in the main canvas.
- Exceeding 8 visuals on a single page degrades Power BI render performance and overwhelms executive comprehension; split deeper exploration onto dedicated detail pages.

## Executive Layout and Visual Hierarchy
- Follow a **Z-pattern reading flow**: place report title and slicers at top/left, headline KPI cards across the top row, primary trend/comparison charts in the middle focal area, and supporting breakdowns or detail tables along the bottom.
- Maintain consistent 12–16 px gutters and align all visual edges to a 12-column responsive grid (1280 x 720 standard 16:9 Power BI canvas).
- Keep at least 15–20% clean whitespace across the canvas; disable redundant legends when direct data labels or single-series titles already identify the metric.

## Slicer and Filter Hygiene
- Limit visible on-page slicers to at most 3–4 high-value dimensions (Date range, Region, Category/Department) and move secondary filters to the Power BI Filter Pane.
